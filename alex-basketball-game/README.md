# Alex: One Day at a Time

A separate Godot 4 prototype for a 2D narrative basketball game.

Alex is 29 and has just graduated. The first playable slice covers one day: morning basketball practice, midday rest or study, afternoon time with his mother or a friend, and an evening match or story choice. Actions affect physical energy, mental focus, mood, basketball skill, and relationship values.

## Planned next steps
- Virtual joystick and on-court movement
- Dribbling, passing, shooting, and ball physics
- Character art and gradual visual/emotional progression
- Branching dialogue and relationship scenes
- Save/load and Android APK export

This folder is intentionally separate from the existing VELTRIX Android app.
